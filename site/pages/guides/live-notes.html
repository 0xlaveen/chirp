Take Live Notes

Keep the mic open for a meeting, a lecture or thinking out loud. Chirp writes Markdown notes as you talk — headings, bullets, to-dos, decisions and open questions — right beside the full transcript.

Needs: Chirp on your Mac with a clean-up modelAlso on: Android

Start and stop

  1. Press ⌃⌥N

    From anywhere. The Chirp Notes window comes forward without stealing focus from the app you're in. You can also choose Start Notes in the menu bar, or press Record in the window.

  2. Talk

    Speech is transcribed in chunks. At most every 15 seconds the local model turns the newest stretch into notes; earlier sections stay put so they don't shift while you read.

  3. Stop

    Press Stop in the window. Chirp finishes the last bit and tidies the notes once more.

Notes need the clean-up model.

Structuring uses the same local model as clean-up. Without it, Notes still works as a live transcript and a plain editor. Download one under Settings → Server → Manage Local Models….

Edit while it writes

The notes are one Markdown document you can type in at any time — before, during or after recording.

  • The tinted block at the end is what the model may still rewrite. Edit anything in it and it becomes yours — the tint goes away and Chirp never touches it again.
  • Your text is safe: updates never overwrite your typing or move your cursor. Each update is one undo step.
  • Markdown shortcuts: # headings, - bullets (Tab to nest), - [ ] to-dos you can click, ⌘B and ⌘I. Preview shows it rendered.
  • Hearing, under the editor, shows words that aren't in the notes yet.

Organize

Every few minutes while you record, Chirp regroups recent notes: duplicate headings merge, and tasks, decisions and questions are collected — without dropping or adding anything (the server checks). Press Organize (⌥⌘R) to do it yourself; an Undo chip appears for 20 seconds. On the reference Mac an update takes about 2 seconds for a short stretch and 5 seconds for a long one.

Label speakers (optional)

Switch on Label speakers in the window's footer and Chirp marks who's talking as Person 1, Person 2… so notes can say who owns each action. It asks before downloading a 106 MB on-device model the first time, handles up to four people, and runs entirely on your Mac.

Save or copy

  • Copy Markdown copies the notes as edited.
  • Save… writes the notes and the transcript to a .md file you choose.
  • Keep saving changes to this file, in the Save panel, keeps that file up to date as the notes change.
  • Closing the window or quitting with unsaved notes asks Save / Discard / Cancel first.

If the Mac crashes

Until you save, Chirp keeps one recovery copy of the notes and transcript on your Mac — text only, never audio, readable only by you. After a crash or power cut, the next launch offers Recovered unsaved notes — Keep · Save… · Discard. Saving or discarding deletes the copy. To keep notes in memory only, turn off Settings → General → Keep a recovery copy until notes are saved.

One at a time.

While Notes is recording, the dictation hotkey is paused on that Mac, and a phone dictating to the same Mac may be told it's busy.

Live Notes on Android

Chirp on Android has the same Live Notes, structured by your Mac over your tailnet. Long-press the Chirp icon and choose Notes, then tap Record. Recording keeps going with the screen off (a notification shows it, with Stop). On an unfolded Fold the transcript sits beside the notes. Copy or Share to keep them — on the phone the notes live in memory only.
